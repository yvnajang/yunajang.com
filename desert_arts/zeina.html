Lot-X

Date: May 12 - Aug 18, 2025

Genre: Immersive Art Experience

Lot-X is a retro-futuristic drive-in experience that reimagines Palm Springs’ iconic car culture with immersive art, fashion, and music. After dark, the desert comes alive with interactive installations, a drive-thru art tunnel, runway shows, and electrifying performances, blending nostalgia with a bold, neon-lit future.
