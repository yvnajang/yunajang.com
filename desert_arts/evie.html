Canvas & Dune

Date: May 12 - Aug 18, 2025

Genre: Cafe/Bar and Gallery

"Canvas & Dune" is an immersive art and design destination in the desert that blends gallery exhibitions, a serene coffee lounge, and a sultry jazz bar into a seamless day-to-night experience. Prioritizing atmosphere and artistry over mass production, the space features locally crafted wooden vessels, artist-designed dishware, and thoughtful programming that turns the desert itself into part of the canvas.
