Palm & Jazz

Date: May 23 - Aug 8, 2025

Genre: Star Gazing & Sunset Viewing

Nestled in a serene desert landscape, this open-air jazz bar offers an unforgettable blend of music, comfort, and natural beauty. Guests lounge in cozy chairs shaded by stylish umbrellas, sipping cocktails as smooth live jazz melodies drift through the air. By day, the venue is perfect for relaxing while watching the sun rise or set over the horizon, casting golden light across the sand. By night, it transforms into a magical stargazing retreat—clear skies reveal constellations overhead as the soft glow of lanterns and the soulful sounds of saxophones create an atmosphere of calm wonder.
