Lost Signals

Date: July 8 - Sep 28, 2025

Genre: Installation Art

Venture into the surreal expanse of the Palm Springs desert and discover Lost Signals, an immersive light installation that blurs the line between technology and nature, isolation and connection. Set against the dramatic backdrop of sand dunes, starry skies, and wind-sculpted landscapes, this hauntingly beautiful experience invites visitors to explore a world where forgotten transmissions flicker to life through beams of color, sound, and movement.
