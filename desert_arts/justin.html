Salvia Apiana

Date: May 12 - Aug 18, 2025

Genre: Shopping & Dining Complex

Salvia Apiana is a Latin phrase meaning white sage, a fragrant plant native to the Palm Springs desert. This venue blends a curated perfume experience with scent-curated snacks and cocktails, which are all rooted in the scents and flavors of local aromatic plants and natural environment of Palm Springs.
